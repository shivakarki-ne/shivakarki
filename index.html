About

Shiva Karki works across three disciplines that most people keep separate: graphic design, video editing, and music production. He treats them as one practice — a headline is a beat, a cut is a brushstroke, a colour palette is a chord.

His process starts with constraints, not inspiration: a brand's tone, a track's tempo, a client's deadline. Everything visual or audible gets built to serve the story, never the other way round.

Currently open to freelance projects in branding, short-form video, and original music for media.
